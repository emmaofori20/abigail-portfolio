About the researcher

My research experience connects fieldwork, spatial analysis and collaborative landscape research in Ghana.

Within the Deforestation-Free Cocoa Project, I led field-data collection, supervised training-coordinate collection for historical mapping, and developed Python machine-learning and CNN classifiers. I also constructed physical 3D landscape models for stakeholder communication.

Through FRAME-Cocoa research, I contributed to soil and foliar sampling, multispectral predictor development, and GIS-based characterisation of shade conditions in cocoa farms. My work distinguishes these individual contributions from the broader teams’ agroforestry and landscape research.

MSc research · Juaboso, Ghana

Cocoa nutrient assessment with UAV imagery

Investigating soil–leaf nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium relationships, and the use of multispectral predictors to estimate cocoa foliar nutrients.

Part of FRAME-Cocoa, sponsored by the African Plant Nutrition Institute.

My contribution

UAV and field-data work, multispectral predictor preparation, feature screening, soil–leaf analysis and comparative machine-learning modelling.

Research finding

Spectral predictions were generally weak and constrained by limited nutrient variability. Soil-to-leaf Random Forest models showed stronger fits for phosphorus and potassium than nitrogen.

Submitted thesis, 2025; related manuscript in preparation

FRAME-Cocoa · Juaboso District, Ghana

Shade canopy in cocoa agroforestry

Characterising cocoa farms using Sentinel-2 spectral, texture and biophysical predictors. Random Forest canopy-cover predictions were grouped into four shade levels.

My contribution

Field collection, remote sensing and GIS processing, Sentinel-derived predictor development, and Random Forest shade analysis.

The broader team integrated tree-species information and mathematical optimisation for agroforestry management.

Co-authored research; related shade-tree paper published

Deforestation-Free Cocoa Project · Ghana

Satellite time series for cocoa landscape monitoring

Historical land-use and land-cover mapping for Sefwi-Wiawso Bibiani, Asunafo-Asutifi and Juaboso-Bia, using reference data for 2015, 2020 and 2024.

My contribution

Led field-data collection, supervised training-coordinate collection, developed Python machine-learning and CNN classifiers, and supported historical maps and physical 3D landscape models.

Supervisor-led research · Ghana’s High Forest Zone

Land-use and land-cover classification

Research comparing classifiers in a spectrally heterogeneous landscape. Illustrated outputs compare Random Forest, SVM and CNN on Landsat 9 L2 imagery for Juaboso-Bia.

My contribution

Prepared and analysed satellite imagery, worked with training and reference data, and produced and interpreted classification outputs within the broader research.
